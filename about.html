a bit about me

Hi, I'm James. I'm an IT student who started getting into web development about a few years ago. Before that, I honestly didn't know much about how websites actually works I just used them.

I got started through school subjects and then kept going on my own through freeCodeCamp tutorials and a lot of YouTube. It clicked for me when I built my first to-do list app and realized I had made something that actually worked in a browser. That was a really good feeling.

Right now I mostly work with HTML, CSS, and basic JavaScript. I'm still building up my skills, but I'm consistent about it and I genuinely enjoy the process — even the frustrating parts.

I recently completed an internship as an IT Support Intern at Clark Outsourcing, where I got real hands-on experience diagnosing hardware, software, and network issues, and supporting day-to-day IT operations. It gave me a much better picture of how things work in an actual work environment.
